## Prompt (verbatim; the file name in the first line was a scratch path)

```
Image: a restrained, flat, vector-style technical illustration of ONE airport departure route seen from directly above (plan view), landscape 16:7, in the manner of a transport-agency infographic or an architectural site plan. Composition from left to right:
1. At the left a terminal building edge (plain grey blocks) with three aircraft stands; one aircraft sits at the middle stand, a second aircraft is just pushed back at the lower stand with a small tug attached to its nose.
2. One single taxiway leaves the stands and runs to the right in a gentle S-curve; a single continuous thick blue line (#2a78d6) follows exactly the centre of this taxiway from the pushed-back aircraft all the way to the runway.
3. On that same taxiway, just before the runway, four identical small aircraft wait in a straight queue one behind another, nose to tail, ON the blue route; a short white hold-short line is painted across the taxiway in front of the first one.
4. At the far right a long runway running top to bottom with one aircraft rolling for take-off at its start, with a subtle small soft shadow.
All aircraft are the same simple narrow-body silhouette, mid-dark grey (#52514e), drawn crisply with no smudging. Background warm off-white (#fcfcfb); taxiways and aprons light warm grey (#e3e2db) with fine slightly darker edges (#cfcec6); runway slightly darker grey (#b9b8af) with white markings. Accent colours used very sparingly: the blue route, a few small orange (#eb6834) dots between the queued aircraft, one small aqua (#1baf7a) dot on the runway at the take-off point. Generous clean margins, no gradients, no glossy 3D, no texture, no neon. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT, letters, numbers, logos or labels anywhere in the image. No people, no vehicles other than the one small tug.
```

Three prompts were tried and the figure kept is the best of the last two (the first had an unclear queue and a cluttered apron). The picture was kept as generated, with no retouching.
